Who we are

The NewTown Classes ("we", "us") runs coaching classes in Newtown, Kolkata and the website thenewtownclasses.com, including the Online Test Series and the student, teacher and admin portals. This policy explains how we handle personal information. For any privacy question: Email thenewtownclasses@gmail.com or call / WhatsApp +91 99034 61361.

What we collect
  • Account details: your name and email from Google sign-in, or the Student ID we give enrolled students.
  • Profile details you enter: class, board, subjects, school, mobile number, date of birth, address, and a parent or guardian’s name, mobile number and email.
  • Test activity: the papers you start, the answer scripts and photos you upload, your marks, feedback and rank.
  • Payments: the amount, what you bought, and the order and payment IDs from Razorpay. We never see or store your card, UPI PIN or bank login — Razorpay handles those.
  • Enquiries: what you write in our contact form.
  • Device basics: your browser stores a few settings and a copy of your recent data so the app opens quickly; we do not use advertising trackers.
Why we use it
  • To give you the tests, check your answer scripts and send results back.
  • To show the right papers for your class, board and subjects.
  • To take payments and keep records of them.
  • To email results to you and your parent or guardian, and to reply to your enquiries.
  • To keep the all-India leaderboard (only your first name and surname initial, e.g. “Riya S.”, is shown to others).
  • To keep the service safe, prevent cheating and fix problems.
Who helps us run the service

We share only what each one needs to do its job:

  • Razorpay — processes payments.
  • Google Firebase — sign-in and our database.
  • Netlify — hosts the website and its server functions.
  • Uploadcare — stores question papers and the answer scripts you upload.
  • OpenAI and Google Gemini — software that helps check answer scripts before a TNC teacher reviews and approves the marks.
  • Brevo — sends result emails.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Students under 18

Most of our students are school students. A parent or guardian should help a student sign up, and by using the service a student confirms a parent or guardian agrees. Parents and guardians can ask us at any time what we hold about their child, or ask us to correct or delete it.

How long we keep it

Checked scripts, marks and results are kept as a permanent record of the student’s progress, unless you ask us to delete them. Payment records are kept as long as the law requires for accounts and tax. Enquiries are kept while we follow up and for a reasonable time after.

Your choices
  • See and update your profile any time from Profile in the Test Series app.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion of your account and results by writing to us.
  • Withdraw consent for result emails to a parent or guardian by asking us.

We reply within a reasonable time. Email thenewtownclasses@gmail.com or call / WhatsApp +91 99034 61361.

Keeping it safe

Access to student data is limited to the student, the institute’s admins and, where needed, the teachers who check the scripts. Our server code checks every payment and every unlock, and data travels over secure (HTTPS) connections. No system is perfect, so please keep your password and Google account secure too.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date below. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
