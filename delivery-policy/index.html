Delivery Policy

Everything we sell online is digital. Here is when you get access.

PrivacyTermsRefundsDeliveryContact
No physical shipping

Everything we sell on this website is digital. Nothing is posted or couriered. You get access in the Test Series app at thenewtownclasses.com/test-series, on the same account you paid from.

When you get access
  • Chapter-wise tests and full mocks: unlocked as soon as the payment is confirmed — usually instantly. The test shows as unlocked under its subject.
  • Weekly mock packs: you join straight away; each mock opens on its scheduled day and time and stays open after that.
  • Custom tests: our teachers prepare your paper within 24 hours of ordering. It then appears under Custom → My tests.
  • Checked answer scripts: we aim to return marks and the checked script within 24 hours of your submission, in Results.
  • Answer keys: where provided, they appear on the test after you submit.
If something hasn’t arrived

Refresh the app first. If a paid item still isn’t there after 30 minutes (or a custom paper after 24 hours), contact us with your Payment ID: Email support@thenewtownclasses.com or call / WhatsApp +91 99034 61361. We will fix it, or refund you as set out in our refund policy.
