Before you enrol

When do new batches start for 2026-27?
New batches for NEET and JEE start in April 2026 for Class 11 students, and April 2026 for Class 12 and droppers. Class 8-10 batches also begin in April. Crash courses are scheduled closer to the exam dates.
What is the batch size for each class?
We keep our batches intentionally small — typically 8 to 10 students — so every student gets personal attention from our faculty and their doubts are addressed promptly.
Is study material included in the fees?
Yes. Comprehensive, regularly updated study material covering NCERT plus advanced topics is provided to all enrolled students. That includes printed notes, DPPs and past year papers at no extra cost.
Do you offer online classes as well?
Yes. We run online, offline and hybrid classes. Students across India can attend live interactive sessions directly through our website, while students in Kolkata can join us at our offline centre.
Are there any scholarships or fee concessions?
Yes. We offer merit-based scholarships for students with strong academic records, and instalment options are available to keep quality coaching accessible.
