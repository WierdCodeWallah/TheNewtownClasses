FAQ

Quick answers.

Everything you need to know before enrolling.

When do new batches start for 2026-27?

New batches for NEET and JEE start in April 2026 for Class 11 students and April 2026 for Class 12 & droppers. Class 8-10 batches begin in April. Crash courses are scheduled closer to exam dates.

What is the batch size for each class?

We keep our batches intentionally small, typically 8-10 students per batch, to ensure every student gets personal attention from our faculty and their doubts are addressed promptly.

Is study material included in the fees?

Yes. Comprehensive, regularly updated study material covering NCERT + advanced topics is provided to all enrolled students. This includes printed notes, DPPs, and past year papers at no extra cost.

Do you offer online classes as well?

Yes. We offer online, offline and hybrid classes. Students across India can attend live interactive sessions directly through our website, while students in Kolkata can join us at our offline centre.

Are there any scholarships or fee concessions available?

Yes, we offer merit-based scholarships for students with strong academic records. Instalment options are also available to make quality coaching accessible.

How can I register or book a free demo class?

Simply fill out the enquiry form in the Contact section, or WhatsApp / call us at +91 9903461361. We will schedule a free demo class and counselling session.
