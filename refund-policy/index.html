Cancellation & Refund Policy

When a purchase can be cancelled or refunded, and how to ask.

PrivacyTermsRefundsDeliveryContact
In short

Purchases on the Online Test Series are non-refundable once paid, because the paper is digital and opens immediately. We do refund in the cases below, where something went wrong on our side.

When we refund
  • You were charged twice for the same test, pack or custom order — we refund the extra payment.
  • Money was deducted but nothing unlocked within 24 hours and we cannot fix it — we unlock it or refund you, as you prefer.
  • We cancel or withdraw a paid paper or mock pack before you have used it — full refund, or a replacement if you prefer.
  • Your custom test paper is not delivered within the promised 24 hours of ordering and you no longer want it — full refund on request.
When we cannot refund
  • You changed your mind after paying, or bought the wrong subject or class.
  • You started a test or opened its paper.
  • Mock pack mocks you missed — each mock stays open after its time, so you can still take it.
  • Purchases made with a promo code or the free mock (nothing was paid).
Cancellation

A purchase cannot be cancelled after payment. A mock pack cannot be cancelled part-way through. If a failed payment left an amount on hold, your bank releases it automatically — usually within 5–7 working days.

How to ask for a refund
  • Email thenewtownclasses@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 99034 61361 within 7 days of the payment.
  • Include your name, the registered email or Student ID, what you bought and the Razorpay Payment ID (from your Razorpay receipt or the payment screen).
  • We reply within 2 working days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay, usually within 5–7 working days.
Classroom coaching

This policy covers online purchases on this website. Fees for classroom (offline) coaching follow the terms shared at admission.
