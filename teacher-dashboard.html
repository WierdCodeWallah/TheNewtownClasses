⚖️ Negative Marking Settings
Set 0 for no negative marking
Set 0 for no penalty on skipped
Example: If marks per Q = 2, negative for wrong = 0.5 →
40 correct × 2 = 80 | 5 wrong × 0.5 = −2.5 | 5 unattempted × 0 = 0 → Total = 77.5

Drop your PDF or Word file here
or click to browse — supports .pdf, .docx, .txt
📋 Supported Formats
Your file should have questions numbered like Q1, Q.1, 1., 1) etc.
Options should be labelled A) B) C) D) or (a) (b) (c) (d) or a. b. c. d.
Answers can be marked as Answer: A or Ans: B (optional — you can set them after import)
📂 Section Format (auto-detected)
Add section headers before your questions and they'll be auto-detected:
Section A – MCQ (2 marks each)
Section B: Short Answer (3 marks each)
SECTION C - Long Answer (5 marks each)
Supported patterns: Section A, SECTION B:, Section C –, Part 1, Part A
Marks per question can be written as: (2 marks each), (3 marks), – 5 marks

📅 Attendance

Choose board, subject and one or more classes, then load the students and tick who is present today. You can only pick from the subject(s) you teach. Present shows green on each student's calendar and counts towards their monthly total. Students cannot mark their own attendance.

🧑‍🎓 Mark Present — Today

Tick the students who are present today, then Save. Once a student is marked present for the day they're locked (🔒 Already present given) and can't be changed again. Present students show green on their calendar and count towards their attendance.
